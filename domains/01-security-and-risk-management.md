# Domain 1: Security and Risk Management

## Overview
This is the foundation of CISSP and the domain closest to GRC. It explains how an organisation governs security, manages risk, and meets its legal and ethical duties.

## Key Concepts
- The CIA triad: confidentiality, integrity and availability
- Policies, standards and procedures
- Risk assessment and the four risk treatments: accept, avoid, transfer or mitigate
- Compliance and privacy
- Business continuity planning
- Professional ethics

## Real-World Example
A company that holds customer data must follow laws such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 or the GDPR, and must show that it manages risk properly. Security teams do this by keeping risk registers, running risk assessments and training staff on policy.

## Common Frameworks, Tools and Practices
- ISO/IEC 27001
- NIST Risk Management Framework
- COBIT
- GRC platforms: ServiceNow, Archer, OneTrust
