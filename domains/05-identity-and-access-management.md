# Domain 5: Identity and Access Management (IAM)

## Overview
IAM makes sure the right people have the right access to the right resources at the right time.

## Key Concepts
- Identification, authentication, authorisation and accountability
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Access control models: DAC, MAC, RBAC, ABAC
- Single sign-on (SSO) and federation
- The identity lifecycle, from joining an organisation to leaving it
- Privileged access management, since administrator accounts are high-value targets
- Regular access reviews to remove permissions no longer needed

## Real-World Example
A new employee receives access based on their job role, and when they resign their accounts are disabled immediately. This reduces insider risk and supports audits.

## Common Frameworks, Tools and Practices
- Microsoft Entra ID
- Okta
- CyberArk
- SAML and OAuth
- Authenticator apps for MFA
