# Personal Reflection

## Most Interesting Domain: Security and Risk Management

The domain I find most interesting is Security and Risk Management, because it is the heart of Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC), which is the area I am currently training in. Technical tools are important, but every security decision should start with a business question: what are we protecting, what could go wrong, and how much risk is acceptable? This domain answers those questions and connects technology to the goals of the organisation.

## Why GRC

I want to specialise in GRC because it combines policy, law, audit and business thinking with cybersecurity. I enjoy structured work such as assessing risks, writing clear policies and checking that controls are followed. I also see strong demand for it in Nigeria and worldwide, as data protection laws and industry rules such as those for banking and fintech keep growing.

## Career Opportunities

- GRC analyst
- Risk analyst
- Compliance officer
- IT auditor
- Information security officer
- Privacy or data protection officer
- Third-party risk manager
- Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), later in a career

**Supporting certifications:** ISC2 CC, ISO 27001 Lead Implementer or Auditor, CISA, CRISC and CISSP.

## Skills to Develop

1. **Frameworks and law:** ISO 27001, NIST CSF, NIST SP 800-53, and relevant laws.
2. **Writing and communication:** explaining risk clearly to managers who are not technical.
3. **Analytical skills:** risk assessment and audit evidence.
4. **Basic technical knowledge:** networks, cloud and access control, so that I understand the controls I review.
5. **Character and habits:** integrity, attention to detail and continuous learning.
